Drone photography & video

A useful output, not just a drone flight.

Not every job needs photogrammetry. Sometimes the useful thing is simply a strong aerial image that shows where a property sits, how a lifestyle block is laid out, or how buildings and land relate to one another.

Plotbird keeps this side of the business deliberately straightforward: capture the useful angles, deliver clean files, and avoid turning a small property shoot into a giant media package unless that is actually what you need.

Aerial photography can work alongside a property listing, website, farm profile, project record or other existing media. Flights remain subject to local airspace, permissions, weather and safe operating conditions.

  • Real-estate aerial stills
  • Lifestyle and rural property imagery
  • Short aerial video clips
  • Property-context and access views
  • High-resolution files for web and marketing
Important: Plotbird provides visual mapping, imagery and photogrammetry. It does not replace licensed cadastral or engineering survey work where that is required.

Questions

Before the drone goes up.

Do you photograph house interiors too?

Plotbird's focus is aerial work. If a project needs a full interior real-estate photography package, it may make more sense to combine Plotbird with a dedicated property photographer.

Can you fly anywhere in Christchurch?

No operator can simply fly anywhere without considering airspace and the applicable rules. Plotbird checks each proposed location and only confirms work that can be conducted appropriately.

How quickly are images delivered?

Turnaround depends on the job and editing required. For a simple stills job, the expected delivery timing is agreed before the flight.
