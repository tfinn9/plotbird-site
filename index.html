About Plotbird

Small by design.

Plotbird is a Christchurch-based aerial mapping and imagery service for property-sized jobs. The focus is simple: useful outputs, sensible scope, and work that doesn’t need an enterprise sales call attached to it.

That might mean mapping a lifestyle block, building a 3D model of a property, documenting a farm from above, or capturing clean aerial media for a listing.

Common questions

What does a small drone mapping job actually involve?

Plotbird is aimed at practical property-sized work. The exact flight and output depend on the site, airspace, weather and what you need to do with the result.

What is drone mapping?

Drone mapping uses a planned set of overlapping aerial photographs to build a detailed, top-down orthomosaic of a property or site. It is useful when one ordinary aerial photo does not provide enough detail or consistency.

Can you create a 3D model of a property?

Yes. Photogrammetry can turn overlapping images into a 3D representation of buildings, terrain or a defined site. The suitability and level of detail depend on the property and the intended use.

Do drone maps replace a licensed survey?

No. Plotbird's mapping is for visual documentation, planning context and imagery. If you need cadastral boundaries, legal measurements or engineering survey accuracy, you need the appropriate licensed professional.

Where does Plotbird work?

Plotbird is based in Christchurch and focuses on Christchurch and Canterbury. Every job is checked for practical access, airspace requirements, weather and the permissions needed for the proposed flight.
